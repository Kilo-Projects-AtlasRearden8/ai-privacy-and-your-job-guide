# Never paste patient or payroll info

There is one rule in this guide that matters more than all the others combined:

**Never paste patient information, payroll information, or any other confidential work data into an AI chat.**

Not "just this once." Not "I'll remove the name." Not "it's probably fine." Never.

Here is why, and what to do instead.

## 1. Patient information (health data)

If you work in healthcare — as a nurse, medical assistant, administrator, or any other role — patient information is protected by law (in the U.S., that includes HIPAA). Pasting a patient's name, diagnosis, treatment, photo, or any detail that could identify them into an AI chat can violate that law and your employer's policies.

Even if you remove the name, small details together can still identify someone — their age, town, rare condition, and shift could be enough. AI chats are not a secure place for health data.

**Examples of what NOT to paste:**
- Patient names, birthdays, medical record numbers
- Diagnoses, medications, treatment plans
- Photos of patients or their wristbands, labels, or paperwork
- Anything that could identify a specific patient, even without a name

## 2. Payroll information

Payroll data includes:

- Social Security numbers
- Salaries, wages, and bonuses
- Bank account details
- Tax forms (W-2s, etc.)
- Pay stubs or direct-deposit information
- Other employees' personal or compensation information

Pasting this into an AI chat exposes it outside your employer's systems. Payroll data is a top target for identity theft and fraud, and leaking it can hurt your coworkers and cost your employer serious money.

## 3. Other data that does not belong in AI

Beyond patients and payroll, never paste:

- Customer or client lists and contact details
- Trade secrets, pricing, or unreleased product plans
- Passwords, login credentials, or security codes
- Your own government ID numbers (unless you fully understand the risk)
- Anything covered by a nondisclosure agreement

## 4. What to do instead

If you want help from AI with a real work situation, use a made-up example instead:

- Instead of a real patient: "A 60-year-old patient with diabetes who is nervous about starting insulin — how do I explain it clearly?"
- Instead of a real payroll issue: "An employee noticed their paycheck is short by $200. What's a polite way to ask HR about it?"
- Instead of real customer data: "A boutique sold 150 candles last month. How should I track inventory?"

Change names, dates, numbers, and locations so no real person could be identified.

## If you already pasted something sensitive

Don't panic — but act quickly:

1. Delete the chat from your history right away
2. Tell your supervisor or IT/compliance team what happened (it is almost always better to report it yourself than to be found out later)
3. Follow your employer's incident reporting process
4. Learn from it and use made-up examples going forward

## Quick checklist for this section

- [ ] I have never pasted patient information into an AI chat
- [ ] I have never pasted payroll or salary information into an AI chat
- [ ] I use made-up, de-identified examples when I ask AI for help with work situations
- [ ] I know who to tell at work if I accidentally paste something sensitive
