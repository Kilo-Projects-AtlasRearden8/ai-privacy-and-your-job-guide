# AI Privacy at Work: A Beginner Manual

Keep your work and health data private when using AI

A plain-English, step-by-step guide for anyone who uses AI on the job — no technical background required.

Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. Education only, not legal advice.

## About this guide

AI tools can be a real help at work: drafting emails, summarizing meetings, organizing schedules, and explaining tricky topics in plain language. But anything you paste into an AI chat can be stored, reviewed by people, or used to train future models — and if you're on a work device or network, your employer may be able to see it.

This manual walks you through, one step at a time:

- What employers can see when you use AI at work
- How to opt out of training where possible
- Why you should never paste patient or payroll information into AI
- How to share information safely
- A printable workplace checklist

## Who this is for

You don't need to be technical. This guide is written for nurses, teachers, small-business owners, retail workers, office staff, freelancers, and anyone else wondering, "Is it safe to paste this into an AI chat?" If that's you, you're in the right place. Nothing here talks down to you — it's just plain English, like a friend explaining it.

## Important: education only, not legal advice

This guide is for educational purposes only. It is not legal advice. It is not a substitute for your employer's policies, HIPAA guidance, or advice from a qualified attorney or compliance professional. Laws and workplace rules vary by location, industry, and employer. Before using AI with sensitive data, check with your employer's IT, compliance, or legal team.
