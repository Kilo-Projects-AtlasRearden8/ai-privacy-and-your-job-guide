# AI Privacy at Work: A Beginner Manual

A plain-English guide to keeping work and health data private when using AI — what employers can see, opting out of training, never pasting patient or payroll info, safe sharing, and a workplace checklist.

Published by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and guides. Indiana, formed October 2026.

## What's inside

- `docs/` — the beginner manual in Markdown
- `site/` — a static HTML version of the same manual (no build step required; open `site/index.html` in any browser)
- `scripts/verify-site.py` — checks the HTML files parse and all internal links resolve

## Topics

1. What employers can see
2. Opting out of training
3. Never paste patient or payroll info
4. Safe sharing
5. Workplace checklist

## Important: education only, not legal advice

This guide is educational only. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for your employer's policies, HIPAA guidance, or advice from a qualified attorney or compliance professional. Before using AI with sensitive data, check with your employer's IT, compliance, or legal team.
