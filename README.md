# AI Privacy at Work: A Beginner Manual

Keep your work and health data private when using AI

A plain-English, step-by-step guide for anyone who uses AI on the job — no technical background required.

**Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and guides. Indiana, formed October 2026.

Written in the spirit of Dylan Asdale's book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io: AI explained "like a friend would" — plain English, step by step, never condescending.

## What's inside

This beginner manual covers five topics:

1. **What employers can see** — company devices, work networks, work-provided AI tools, personal accounts
2. **Opting out of training** — where to find the setting, enterprise vs. free accounts, what opting out does and doesn't protect
3. **Never paste patient or payroll info** — health data (HIPAA context), payroll data, what to do instead, what to do if you already pasted something
4. **Safe sharing** — what's usually okay, what to keep out, de-identification, approved tools, keeping work and personal separate
5. **Workplace checklist** — a printable before/during/after/incident checklist

Plus an education-only disclaimer.

If that's you, you're in the right place. Nothing here talks down to you — it's just plain English, like a friend explaining it.

## Important: education only, not legal advice

This guide is for educational purposes only. It is **not legal advice**. It does not create an attorney-client relationship. It is not a substitute for:

- Your employer's AI, data security, or confidentiality policies
- HIPAA or other healthcare privacy guidance
- Advice from a qualified attorney, compliance professional, or your employer's legal team

Laws and workplace rules vary by location, industry, and employer. Before using AI with sensitive data at work, check with your employer's IT, compliance, or legal team.
