Opting out of training

Many AI companies use conversations to improve and train their future models. That means something you paste today could help train a model that other people use tomorrow.

1. Check your settings

In most consumer AI tools, there is a setting — often called "Data controls," "Train on my content," or "Improve the model for everyone" — that lets you opt out of having your chats used for training.

Turn it off for any personal AI account you use for work-related ideas, and any account where you might accidentally paste sensitive information.

Where to look: settings → data privacy → training / model improvement. Every tool names it differently, so if you can't find it, search the tool's help center for "opt out of training."

2. Enterprise vs. free accounts

Work-provided AI accounts (enterprise plans) often already exclude your data from training by contract — but not always. Check with your IT team rather than assuming.

Free personal accounts are more likely to use your chats for training unless you opt out.

3. Opting out is not always retroactive

Turning off training today usually stops future chats from being used. It does not always delete chats you already had. If you already pasted something sensitive:

  • Delete the chat from your history if the tool allows it
  • Check the tool's data retention policy to see how long chats are kept
  • Contact the tool's support team if you need help deleting data

4. "Opt out" does not mean "safe for secrets"

Even with training turned off, your chats may still be stored by the AI company for a period of time, reviewed by human contractors for safety checks, or accessible to your employer if you're on a work device or network.

Opting out is a layer of protection, not a reason to paste patient records or payroll data.

Quick checklist for this section

  • I checked the training settings on my personal AI accounts and turned training off where available
  • I asked IT whether my work-provided AI account excludes data from training
  • I know how to delete a chat if I paste something I shouldn't have
  • I still treat every AI chat as potentially stored or seen by others
