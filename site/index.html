AI Privacy at Work: A Beginner Manual

Keep your work and health data private when using AI — plain English, step by step, no technical background required. Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. Education only, not legal advice.

About this guide

AI tools can be a real help at work: drafting emails, summarizing meetings, organizing schedules, and explaining tricky topics in plain language. But anything you paste into an AI chat can be stored, reviewed by people, or used to train future models — and if you're on a work device or network, your employer may be able to see it.

This manual walks you through, one step at a time:

  • What employers can see
  • Opting out of training
  • Never paste patient or payroll info
  • Safe sharing
  • Workplace checklist

Who this is for

You don't need to be technical. This guide is written for nurses, teachers, small-business owners, retail workers, office staff, freelancers, and anyone else wondering, "Is it safe to paste this into an AI chat?" If that's you, you're in the right place. Nothing here talks down to you — it's just plain English, like a friend explaining it.

Important: education only, not legal advice. This guide is for educational purposes only. It is not legal advice and is not a substitute for your employer's policies, HIPAA guidance, or advice from a qualified attorney or compliance professional. Before using AI with sensitive data, check with your employer's IT, compliance, or legal team.

Where to start

Start with What employers can see, then work through the rest of the guide in order. The Workplace checklist at the end is designed to print or save and keep handy at your desk.
