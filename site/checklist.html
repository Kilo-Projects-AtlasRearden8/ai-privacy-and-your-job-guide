Workplace checklist

Print this out, save it to your desktop, or bookmark this page. Keep it handy whenever you use AI at work.

Before you start

  • I know my employer's AI policy (I asked IT, compliance, or my supervisor if I wasn't sure)
  • I am using an AI tool my employer allows for work tasks
  • I know whether my chats can be seen by my employer's admin
  • I turned off "train on my content" in my personal AI accounts, where available
  • I am on a network I trust (I avoid pasting sensitive info on public Wi-Fi)

While you work

  • No patient names, diagnoses, or health details are in the chat
  • No payroll data, salaries, Social Security numbers, or bank details are in the chat
  • No passwords, login credentials, or security codes are in the chat
  • No customer lists, contracts, trade secrets, or NDA-covered info is in the chat
  • Any real situation I discuss uses made-up names, dates, and details
  • I am not sharing my screen with AI chat visible during a meeting

When you finish

  • I deleted chats I no longer need
  • I logged out of AI tools on shared or public devices
  • I closed AI tabs when stepping away from my work device

If something goes wrong

  • I know who to tell at work if I accidentally paste something sensitive (supervisor, IT, or compliance)
  • I will report it right away instead of hoping no one notices

Keep this checklist near your workstation. When in doubt: don't paste it, and ask first.
